The Shop

A steady chair on Johnson Ave

Gentlemen’s Barbershop is a neighbourhood barbershop in East Williamsburg, a few blocks off the Montrose Ave stop. The work is classic barbering — clippers, shears and the straight razor — done carefully and without rushing you out of the chair.

Precision scissor work and skin fades are the everyday, and the shop keeps long-hair specialists on the floor for cuts that most barbers would rather not take on.

It is the kind of room people come back to: quiet enough to hear yourself think, warm enough to stay a while, and detailed enough that the cut still sits right two weeks later. An easy walk from Bushwick and Greenpoint.
